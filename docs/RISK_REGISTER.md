## PE architecture review — five highest risks

1. **R-007:** INT32 is not mathematically safe for the full-range 512-input case.
2. **R-010:** provider delivery may underfeed the selected P.
3. **R-008/R-026:** local P=16 storage mapping is a synthesis fact, but the
   100 MHz quantization-to-activation-write path fails and F37X mapping remains
   unmeasured.
4. **R-011:** tail masks and runtime dimensions can silently corrupt dot products.
5. **R-012:** multi-channel backpressure and ping-pong ownership can deadlock or
   overwrite data without formal invariants and randomized stall testing.
